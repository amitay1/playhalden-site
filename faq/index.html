The game

What is HALDEN?

A first-person roguelite shooter. You climb a living tower room by room on a branching route, pick up weapons and relics as you go, and fight whatever the tower grows to stop you. When a run ends, you start again from the bottom.

Is the trailer gameplay?

No. The reveal trailer is a cinematic made with AI-assisted visuals. It sets the mood. For what the game looks like today, see the in-engine captures on the home page — they’re labelled. Gameplay footage will follow.

Is the key art what the game looks like?

No. The key art is AI-assisted illustration made to set the tone. The game itself is stylized and cel-shaded; images captioned ‘in-engine’ show the development build as it really is.

Can I play with friends?

That’s the plan. Online co-op is designed in from the start and is in development and testing now, planned for up to four players. Solo runs already play from start to finish in the development build. We’ll call co-op ready when it works between real PCs over the internet.

They haven’t been measured yet. We’ll publish them when they are.
