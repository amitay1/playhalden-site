Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the game HALDEN (the "game") and the website playhalden.com (the "website") collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. HALDEN is made by the developer of HALDEN ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, write to contact@playhalden.com.

1. Information the game collects

  • Sign-in information. HALDEN uses Epic Online Services, operated by Epic Games, for sign-in and online play. When you sign in with an Epic Games account, the game receives your Epic account ID and display name. If you play without an Epic Games account, Epic Online Services creates an anonymous identifier for your PC (a "device ID"), and the game uses the display name you choose in the game.
  • Friends and online status. If you allow it when you sign in with Epic Games, the game reads your Epic friends list and your friends' online status, only to show you which friends are online and to send and receive squad invitations. We do not keep a copy of your friends list.
  • Game data. Your progress, settings and player profile are saved on your PC. A backup copy of your player profile is stored in Epic Online Services cloud storage, linked to your player account, so your progress can be restored.
  • Multiplayer. When you host or join a squad, the game uses Epic Online Services lobbies and network connections between players. Other players in your squad can see your display name, your squad ID and your in-game activity. Connections between players are set up through Epic Online Services and may be relayed through Epic's servers.
  • Crash reports. If the game crashes, a crash report is saved on your PC. It is not sent to us automatically.

The game does not collect payment information, does not show advertising, and does not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How the information is used

  • to sign you in and keep your player identity consistent between sessions;
  • to run co-op play: squads, squad IDs, lobbies, invitations and connections between players;
  • to save and restore your progress and settings;
  • to keep the game working, fix problems and keep play fair.

3. Sharing

Epic Games processes the information described above to provide Epic Online Services to the game. Epic's own handling of your data is described in the Epic Games Privacy Policy. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Retention and deletion

Data saved on your PC stays there until you delete it or uninstall the game. The cloud backup of your player profile is kept while your player account is in use. You can ask us at any time to delete the cloud backup and any other information we hold about you by writing to contact@playhalden.com. Your Epic Games account itself is managed by Epic Games.

5. Children

HALDEN is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its use. To use any of these rights, write to contact@playhalden.com. You can also withdraw the permissions you gave the game at any time from your Epic Games account settings (Apps and Accounts). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Sign-in and online services run over encrypted connections provided by Epic Online Services. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information the game uses.

8. This website (playhalden.com)

This section covers the website itself, not the game.

  • No cookies, no trackers. The website doesn't set cookies, doesn't use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking, and doesn't store anything in your browser.
  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information (such as your browser type and the page you asked for) when you visit, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. GitHub may process this information in the United States and elsewhere.
  • Fonts and images are served from this website. Your browser doesn't contact any font, image or analytics service.
  • Trailer videos. Trailers are hosted on YouTube. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press Play. When you press Play, your browser connects to YouTube (Google) in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). From then on, YouTube may store cookies or similar data on your device and process your IP address and viewing information under the Google Privacy Policy. If you'd rather not, don't press Play. Links marked "Watch on YouTube" and "Vertical cut" open YouTube directly.
  • Links to other sites. Links to YouTube and other services take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Email. If you write to contact@playhalden.com, we receive your email address and your message through our domain's email forwarding and our mailbox provider. We use them only to reply, and we delete them when the conversation no longer needs them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
