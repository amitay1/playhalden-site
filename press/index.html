Short description

HALDEN is a cel-shaded first-person roguelite shooter: climb a living tower through three strata, one branching route at a time. In development for Windows PC.

Description

HALDEN is a first-person roguelite shooter about climbing a living tower. Pick one of four climbers, each with a resource of their own to build and spend, and fight up through three strata — the sunlit ruins of Rootworks, the swallowed temples and castles of Broodways, and the white-gold heights of The Crown. Every run follows a new branching route of fights, elites, events, a market and campfire rests, and every stratum ends in a boss. The tower pays for precision: hits fill your Ichor, headshots fill it faster, and a full well unleashes a SURGE that drags nearby enemies in and leaves them wide open. Along the way you’ll find 30+ weapons, relics, and Companions — small creatures who join you on your next climb. HALDEN is cel-shaded, built in Unreal Engine 5, and in development for Windows PC, with online co-op in development and testing.

Features

  • A living tower to climb: three strata — Rootworks, Broodways and The Crown — each ending in a boss.
  • A new branching route every run: fights, elites, events, a market and campfire rests.
  • Ichor rewards accuracy: hits fill it, headshots fill it faster, and a full well unleashes SURGE.
  • Four climbers who play differently, each with their own resource to build and spend.
  • 30+ weapons — guns, bows, blades, hammers, a chakram, a boomerang and a lantern-book — with burn, shock and poison in the mix.
  • Companions: creatures you meet on one run who climb with you on the next.
  • Online co-op in development, planned for up to four players.

Development status

HALDEN is in active development. The current build plays a full solo run through all three strata, from the first room to the final boss. Online co-op is in development and testing. There is no release date, price or store page yet. The game was revealed with a cinematic trailer on October 3, 2026.
