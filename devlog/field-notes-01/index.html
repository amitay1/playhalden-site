Field notes 01: Where the climb stands

This is the first entry in HALDEN’s devlog: short, honest notes on what’s in the build and what’s next. Here’s where things stand.

What plays today. In the current development build, a solo run climbs all three strata — Rootworks, Broodways and The Crown — from the first room to the final boss. Four climbers are playable, each with a resource of their own to build and spend. More than thirty weapons can drop along the way, alongside relics and gold.

What makes it HALDEN. Ichor. The tower pays for precision: hits fill it, headshots fill it faster, and at 100 you SURGE. Aiming well becomes a resource you can spend. And Kindred — small creatures you meet on one run who climb at your side on the next.

What’s still being built. Online co-op is in development and testing; the next milestone is a squad playing across real PCs over the internet. Models, lighting and effects are still changing, which is why every image on this site says whether it’s in-engine or illustration.

What’s next. Co-op tests between real machines, fresh footage from the current build, and more field notes as the tower grows.
