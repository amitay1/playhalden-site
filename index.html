The climb

One tower. Three strata. The only way out is up.

Every climber thinks they are the first.

HALDEN is a run-based shooter. You start at the foot of a living tower and fight your way up, room by room, choosing your path on a branching map.

Rooms pay out in gold, Ichor and weapons. Elites pay more. Fall, and the run ends — and the tower is waiting at the bottom for your next try.

Tower rule

It keeps count.

Skip a fight and the tower doesn’t forget. What you dodged can come looking for you later in the climb.

The climbers

Pick your climber.

I have eaten better climbers than these.

Four climbers, four ways to fight. Each one builds a resource of their own and spends it to turn a bad room around.

  • The Bulwark

    Bastion Tank

    Hold a lane, brace against frontal attacks, then spend Pressure to strike back.

    Resource: Pressure

  • The Pyro

    Cinder Assault

    Build Heat on burning enemies. Stay hot for faster fire, or vent it to break through.

    Resource: Heat

  • The Conduit

    Lysa Healer

    Mark an enemy, earn Grace by attacking, then choose who receives your healing and wards.

    Resource: Grace

  • The Alchemist

    Vesper Combo

    Prime targets, build Preparation with weapon hits, then spend Reagent to detonate your setup.

    Resource: Reagent

The arsenal

Thirty-odd weapons. No two fight alike.

Take what I drop. Show me what you do with it.

Guns and bows, blades and hammers, a chakram that comes home and a book that sets the room alight. Weapons drop in rising rarity, and burn, shock and poison run through the arsenal.

  • Kitsune

    Hand Cannon

    A fox-fire hand cannon. One heavy round at a time, and each one lands like a verdict.

  • Kamaitachi

    Sickle Boomerang

    Three sickle-blades leave the wrist at once and come home. Brutal up close, slow to return.

  • Bishamon

    War Hammer

    A war-god's hammer. Raise it, break the floor, and hold the quake around you.

  • Raijin

    Ember Bow

    An ember bow. Drawn slow and loosed once — the arrow punches through the line.

  • Chochin

    Lantern-Book

    A lantern-book of fire. Low per-hit, but everything it touches burns.

  • Kagero

    Spirit Chakram

    Returning spirit-chakram. Crackling blades that leap from foe to foe.

  • Tsukuyomi no Te

    Void Cannon

    The moon's own hand. Slow voids that gather the crowd, then collapse.

  • Byakko

    Storm Polearm

    A storm-polearm you throw. It leaves your hand and the next one forms in it.

Ichor

Accuracy pays.

My blood is amber. Spill it well.

The tower bleeds Ichor, and it rewards precision, not noise. Every hit feeds your Ichor. Headshots feed it faster. Fill it to 100 and SURGE: a blast that yanks the nearest enemies in and leaves them wide open. Ichor carries from room to room, so a clean fight sets up the next one.

Kindred

Not everything in the tower wants you dead.

Kindred are small creatures you meet on the climb. Greet one, and from your next run it climbs at your side — one companion per climber. You don’t buy them. You find them.

The Antibodies

The tower fights back.

You are a splinter. I know what to do with splinters.

Everything that comes at you is the tower defending itself. Swarms that flood a room. Hounds that charge. Bulbs that spit. Shield-bearers, hex-casters and bombers. And at the top of every stratum, something much bigger.

Co-op

Built for a squad. Tested before it’s promised.

HALDEN is designed for online co-op, planned for up to four players: shared fights, a squad that covers each other’s blind side, and climbers who pick each other up. Co-op is in development and testing right now. We’ll call it ready when it works between real PCs over the internet — not before.

Status: in testing
